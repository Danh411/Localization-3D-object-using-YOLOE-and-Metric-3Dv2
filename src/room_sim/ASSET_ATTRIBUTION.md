# Attribution for Gazebo assets

The kitchen scene and vendored models derive from
[makerspet/oomwoo_gazebo](https://github.com/makerspet/oomwoo_gazebo/tree/27fe8ecd4b2962744642fbce58e813989eed858f).

meshes were converted from OBJ to GLB with separate original albedo textures.
